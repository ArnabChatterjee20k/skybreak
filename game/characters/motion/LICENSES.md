# Motion sources and licences

Every clip in `motion_library.res` is retargeted from one of these two sets. Neither needs a login.
Skybreak is personal and non-commercial, which CC BY-NC 4.0 allows.

## Sets

- **CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database**, http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu. "The data used in this
  project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF
  EIA-0196217." Terms: free for research and commercial use (READMEFIRST.txt of the BVH release).
  We used the BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed.com, 2010 release), mirrored at
  https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap.
- **Bandai-Namco-Research-Motiondataset-1**, Bandai Namco Research Inc.,
  https://github.com/BandaiNamcoResearchInc/Bandai-Namco-Research-Motiondataset.
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0).
  We changed it: retargeted to Kael's skeleton, trimmed, foot-locked, made in place, looped
  and time-warped.

The flight clips (`hover`, `fly`, `bank_left`, `bank_right`, `dive`) hold the airborne part of
CMU 16_01 (a jump) and add a procedural body pitch and roll, because mocap has no flight. `tech` is
`get_up` played at double speed. Strike windows give the source seconds around the strike
before time-warping.
